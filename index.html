The Local Duel: Mapping the Wealth Profiles of Malaysia and Australia

Australia's billionaire landscape is vast, coastal, and dominated by self-made entrepreneurs in heavy industrial mining and modern software technology. In contrast, Malaysia's billionaire presence is tighter, heavily centered around diversified, inherited family conglomerate structures, and grounded in property and finance sectors.
